# Player Awakening Flow Specification

## 1) Hero’s Journey Structure (Metaphysical Variant)

This structure maps the classic monomyth to an internal journey of expanding awareness. It is the experiential arc the player feels, not a literal sequence of plot points.

- **Call to Awareness**: The journey begins not with an external call, but an internal dissonance—a subtle feeling that the perceived reality is incomplete. A choice or event feels hollow, leading to the first quiet question: "Is this all there is?"

- **Resistance / Ego Friction**: The Physical Mind's defense mechanisms activate. The initial dissonance is met with rationalization, doubt, and a preference for familiar, predictable loops. This is the "refusal of the call," where the player can choose to ignore the initial feeling of unease.

- **First Synchronicity**: After a moment of genuine curiosity or acting on a small, unexplainable impulse (the first "excitement signal"), the engine generates a low-probability, high-meaning coincidence. It is subtle but statistically undeniable—a crack in the "randomness" of the world.

- **Belief Examine Moment**: The synchronicity acts as a catalyst, forcing a moment of reflection. The UI prompts the player to inspect the belief that was active just before the event (e.g., "Nothing ever works out for me") and contrasts it with the objective outcome.

- **Alignment Choice Point**: The player is presented with a clear choice, not between good and evil, but between expansion and contraction. One path is guided by a familiar fear or desire for control (Ego); the other is guided by the nascent excitement signal (Higher Mind).

- **Vibration Shift**: Upon making the aligned choice, the player experiences a direct sensory feedback of a state change. The "noise" in their perception clears, colors become slightly more vivid, and a subtle harmonic resonance is felt. This is the first measurable change in their vibrational state.

- **First Timeline Ripple**: The vibrational shift causes a subtle but tangible change in the immediate reality. An obstacle vanishes, a previously hostile character becomes neutral, a new opportunity appears. It is a direct, observable consequence of their internal alignment.

- **Glimpse Beyond Physical Mind**: The player is granted a momentary, fleeting view of a higher-order mechanic. The UI briefly flashes an overlay of the "Synchronicity Radar" or the "Belief Structure," confirming their suspicion that there is an elegant, underlying system at play.

- **Stabilization / Integration**: The player's core perception model is updated. They can no longer fully return to the "amnesia" state. The basic mechanics of consciousness are now an accepted part of their reality, and the journey shifts from discovery to conscious application.

## 2) Tutorial Phases

This is the mechanical progression that implements the Hero's Journey, gating UI and mechanics to ensure a gradual, discovery-based learning curve.

- **Phase 0 — *Amnesia State***
  - **Player State**: Believes they are a character in a fixed, physical world. Assumes reality is objective and random.
  - **UI State**: Limited to Personality and Physical Mind layers. Shows stats, inventory, and basic interaction prompts. A "fog of assumption" subtly desaturates the visuals and muffles audio.

- **Phase 1 — *Signal Interference***
  - **Player State**: Experiences the "Call to Awareness." Feels the first pull of an "excitement prompt"—a choice that feels inexplicably more compelling than the others.
  - **UI State**: Subtle guidance signals appear. The compelling choice might have a faint glow, a unique audio cue, or a gentle haptic vibration. The first synchronicity event is logged in a previously hidden "Patterns" tab.

- **Phase 2 — *Belief Exposure***
  - **Player State**: Becomes curious about the "why" behind the synchronicity.
  - **UI State**: The "Belief Inspector" UI unlocks in a read-only mode. It highlights the active belief during the synchronicity event, showing the dissonance between belief and outcome without judgment.

- **Phase 3 — *Alignment Exercise***
  - **Player State**: Understands the concept of choice beyond simple logic and is ready to test it.
  - **UI State**: The "Vibration Meter" becomes visible. At a key "Alignment Choice Point," the potential vibrational impact of each choice is shown as a subtle upward or downward arrow on the meter.

- **Phase 4 — *Lens Expansion***
  - **Player State**: Directly experiences the link between internal state and external reality.
  - **UI State**: A limited, read-only version of the Higher-Mind layer becomes accessible. The "Synchronicity Radar" flashes briefly to confirm a resonance, or the "Timeline View" shows a minor branch shimmering into focus after an aligned choice.

- **Phase 5 — *Identity Softening***
  - **Player State**: Begins to intuit that "they" are the consciousness focusing *through* the personality, not the personality itself.
  - **UI State**: Dialogue and internal monologue prompts shift from "What should **I** do?" to reflective questions like "What feels most aligned with the Self I am becoming?"

- **Phase 6 — *First Integration Test***
  - **Player State**: Ready to consciously apply the mechanics to solve a problem.
  - **UI State**: A scenario is presented that has a dense, difficult "physical" solution and a simple, elegant "vibrational" solution. The player must use the Belief Inspector, consult the Vibration Meter, and follow the excitement signal to unlock the elegant path, which results in a clearly visualized timeline branch shift.

- **Phase 7 — *Stabilization***
  - **Player State**: The core mechanics are understood and integrated. The player is ready for free play.
  - **UI State**: The "Awareness OS" is fully installed. The Physical and Higher Mind UI layers are stable and accessible. Tutorial prompts are replaced by a persistent but unobtrusive "Clarity" affordance that the player can access for guidance. "Training wheels mode" is engaged.

## 3) UX Metaphors and Symbols

The UI's symbolic language is designed to be universal, avoiding religious or New Age iconography. It draws from physics, sacred geometry, and data visualization to create a tone that is both profound and analytical.

- **Resistance (Ego Friction)**: Visualized as **_signal noise_** or **_static distortion_**. In the UI, this manifests as a subtle chromatic aberration effect, a slight desaturation of colors, or a low-frequency audio hum. It represents a state of low coherence.

- **Alignment (Clarity/Resonance)**: Represented by **_harmonic wave patterns_** and **_increased signal clarity_**. As alignment increases, the UI becomes sharper, colors become more vibrant and saturated, and the ambient audio resolves into clear, harmonic tones.

- **Synchronicity**: Visualized as a **_golden thread_** or a **_luminous data trail_**. When a synchronicity occurs, the related objects or characters in the environment are briefly connected by a subtle, shimmering line, highlighting the improbable connection between them.

- **Belief Walls vs. Belief Openings**:
  - **Walls**: A limiting belief is represented as a **_semi-transparent, crystalline barrier_** in the UI's belief inspector. The higher the rigidity, the more opaque the crystal.
  - **Openings**: When a belief is softened, the crystal fractures and becomes more translucent, representing an **_aperture_** through which new perceptions can emerge.

- **Blueprint Pull vs. Ego Push**:
  - **Blueprint Pull**: The guidance from the Soul/Higher Mind is visualized as a gentle, **_gravitational lensing effect_** around the aligned choice. It doesn't force the player, but it subtly bends the visual space towards that option.
  - **Ego Push**: A choice driven by fear or control is represented by **_hard, straight vector lines_** that "push" from the player's perspective towards the object of desire or away from the object of fear, signifying a forceful, insistent energy.

## 4) Player Feedback Loops

Feedback is designed to be subtle and diegetic, nudging the player towards self-discovery rather than explicitly telling them what to do. The goal is to make the player *feel* the mechanics before they consciously understand them.

- **Emotional Color**: The overall color palette of the game world subtly shifts to reflect the player's internal emotional state. Fear introduces cooler, blue-gray tones, while joy brings in warmer, golden hues. This is not a simple filter, but an integrated shift in the scene's lighting and grading.

- **Cognitive Hint**: After a significant event, a single, reflective word may appear in a corner of the screen (e.g., "Resonance," "Dissonance," "Contraction," "Expansion"). It provides a label for the feeling without a lengthy explanation.

- **Pattern Recognition Prompt**: The UI doesn't show raw stats. Instead, after a recurring pattern of behavior, it might prompt: "Three times you have chosen [X], and each time the outcome was [Y]. A pattern is emerging."

- **Synchronicity Marker**: When a synchronicity occurs, a small, elegant glyph (e.g., a stylized Vesica Piscis) briefly appears over the involved elements. This marker is also added to the "Patterns" log for later review, allowing the player to connect the dots themselves.

- **Vibration Meter Shift**: The primary feedback for choice. Instead of a number, the meter is a visual representation of a waveform. An aligned choice makes the wave more coherent and harmonic; a misaligned choice makes it more chaotic and noisy.

- **Timeline Subtle-branch Cue**: The player is not explicitly told they have shifted timelines. Instead, they might notice a subtle change in the environment after an important choice—a book has a different title, a flower has changed color, a line of dialogue is slightly altered. This cues them to the fact that their reality is fluid and responsive.

## 5) UI State Boundaries

The principle of **progressive disclosure** is critical. The player should only see the UI elements relevant to their current level of awareness to avoid overwhelm and maintain the sense of discovery.

| Tutorial Phase | Newly Visible UI Elements | Newly Unlocked Functionality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 0: Amnesia** | `PersonalityHUD` (stats, etc.), `InteractionPrompts` | Basic world interaction. |
| **Phase 1: Interference** | `ExcitementGlow` (on choices), `PatternsLog` (hidden tab) | Excitement signal appears; synchronicities are logged. |
| **Phase 2: Exposure** | `BeliefInspector` (panel) | Can view active beliefs in read-only mode. |
| **Phase 3: Alignment** | `VibrationMeter` (waveform display) | Can see the potential vibrational impact of choices. |
| **Phase 4: Expansion** | `HigherMindGlimpse` (temporary overlays) | `SynchronicityRadar` or `TimelineTree` flash briefly. |
| **Phase 5: Softening** | `SelfReflectionPrompts` (in dialogue) | Dialogue shifts to more introspective questions. |
| **Phase 6: Integration** | `FullBeliefInspector` (interactive) | Can now consciously work with and reframe beliefs. |
| **Phase 7: Stabilization**| `AwarenessOS_HUD` (stable HM/PM views) | All core mechanics are now visible and usable. |

Higher layers (Soul, Oversoul) remain completely hidden until they are earned much later in the full game experience. The debug overlays from the `TimelineVisualDebugSpec` are never shown to the player by default.

## 6) Dialogue / Voice Principles

The narrative voice, whether delivered through a mentor character, UI text, or internal monologue, is a crucial part of the onboarding. It is not a teacher, but a fellow traveler slightly ahead on the path.

- **Mentor Tone but Not Authoritarian**: The voice is calm, wise, and knowing, but never condescending or dictatorial. It offers observations, not commands.
  - **Instead of**: "You must choose the path of excitement."
  - **Use**: "You noticed one path felt more resonant. What happens if you follow that feeling?"

- **Reflective Questions**: The primary mode of interaction is the question. The goal is to prompt the player's own inner inquiry, not to provide answers.
  - "What did that choice feel like in your body?"
  - "What belief might be creating this recurring pattern?"
  - "Notice what just changed in your environment after you made that choice."

- **Curiosity Invitations**: The language is framed as an invitation to explore, experiment, and be curious about one's own consciousness.
  - "Let's try a small experiment with belief."
  - "What if you approached this situation with no expectation?"
  - "Simply observe the connection between your inner state and the outer world."

- **Empower Agency**: The voice constantly reinforces that the player is the one in control, the creator of their experience. It cedes authority to the player's own inner wisdom.
  - "Your inner guidance is the ultimate authority here."
  - "Trust the feeling. It is your most reliable compass."
  - "You are the one focusing this consciousness. You decide where it goes."

## 7) Failsafes & Guardrails

The system is designed to be inherently safe and supportive. There is no "losing" in the traditional sense; there is only learning through contrast.

- **Never Punish Failure — Only Density / Opacity**: If a player consistently makes choices that lead to dissonance, they are not penalized with damage or loss of resources. Instead, their perceptual field becomes more "dense." The UI's signal noise increases, synchronicities become rarer, and the world feels more opaque and resistant. This is a natural consequence of their chosen vibration, not a punishment.

- **Dissonance Increases Learning Potential**: A state of high dissonance is framed as a powerful learning opportunity. The greater the contrast, the clearer the eventual shift to alignment will feel. The narrative voice may gently prompt: "The resistance is high here. A powerful belief must be present. A great opportunity for clarity."

- **Player Always Returns to Clarity Affordance**: No matter how dense the player's experience becomes, there is always a simple, accessible "clarity affordance." This could be a meditative state they can enter, a specific location in the world, or a mentor character they can visit. This space temporarily quiets the ego friction and allows them to reconnect with the Higher Mind's signal, ensuring no player can get permanently "stuck."

## 8) Testing Scenarios

The onboarding flow must be robust enough to handle various player archetypes and mindsets.

- **Player Resistant to Awakening (The Skeptic)**:
  - **Behavior**: Deliberately ignores excitement prompts, chooses the most "logical" or cynical path, dismisses synchronicities as pure coincidence.
  - **System Response**: The engine does not force the awakening. The player's reality becomes more mundane and statistically probable. The "Call to Awareness" will re-emerge later in a different, perhaps more undeniable, form. The test is to ensure the system respects the player's free will to say "no."

- **Player Curious but Confused (The Seeker)**:
  - **Behavior**: Wants to understand but is overwhelmed by the concepts. Tries to follow the path but often gets distracted or misinterprets the signals.
  - **System Response**: The narrative voice provides gentle, clarifying questions. The "Patterns" log becomes crucial, allowing the player to review past events and connect the dots at their own pace. The system must be patient and provide multiple, redundant clues.

- **Player Eager but Distractible (The Enthusiast)**:
  - **Behavior**: Chases every synchronicity and excitement signal without integrating the lessons. Their vibrational state is high but unstable.
  - **System Response**: The system introduces scenarios that require focus and stability. The player might find that their high-excitement state without grounding leads to chaotic, unpredictable outcomes, gently teaching them the importance of integration and deliberate choice.

- **Player Mechanical/Completionist Type (The Gamer)**:
  - **Behavior**: Tries to "game" the system by always choosing the option with the highest "vibration" score, treating it as a stat to be maximized rather than a feeling to be embodied.
  - **System Response**: The system introduces choices where the highest immediate vibrational payoff leads to a less interesting or more complicated long-term outcome. This teaches the player that alignment is not about min-maxing a single metric but about holistically following a resonant path.

- **Player Tries to Break the System (The Hacker)**:
  - **Behavior**: Deliberately acts in contradictory ways, tries to find logical loopholes, and tests the boundaries of the simulation.
  - **System Response**: The system must be logically consistent. The law of "what you put out is what you get back" is applied rigorously. If the player puts out chaotic, contradictory energy, their reality becomes chaotic and contradictory. This demonstrates the system's integrity by perfectly mirroring the player's own state.

## 9) Success Criteria

The onboarding flow is successful if, by the end of the sequence, the player has achieved the following states of awareness. These are measured through behavioral observation and post-tutorial A/B testing, not through explicit quizzes.

- **Player realizes *they* are the focus of experience**: The player's internal monologue and choices demonstrate an understanding that they are not the personality-construct, but the consciousness experiencing reality *through* that construct. They have shifted from "I am this character" to "I am focusing my awareness here."

- **Understands **why** belief/feeling/choice matter**: The player can articulate, in their own terms, the causal link between their internal state (beliefs and emotions) and their external reality. They see it not as a mystical or arbitrary rule, but as the core operational physics of the simulation.

- **Recognizes synchronicity as system feedback**: The player stops seeing synchronicities as random, fortunate coincidences and starts treating them as intentional, meaningful signals from a higher intelligence within the system— a cosmic breadcrumb trail.

- **Is ready for free play and deeper layers**: The player has integrated the foundational mechanics to the point where they can navigate the game world without constant hand-holding. They are equipped with the "Awareness OS" and are now curious and empowered to explore the deeper layers of consciousness (Soul and Oversoul) that the main game will offer.
